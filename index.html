Downtown Session Web

Nine albums from the New York downtown scene, 1980–83, and the musicians who played on them. Each musician has one color, and a line in that color runs to every album they appear on.

Hover or tap a musician, an album, or a name in the list below to follow their lines.

Everyone on these records

Performers only; producers and engineers are left out. Kip Hanrahan's 1981 debut is titled Coup de Tête, and the credits here come from its Discogs listing, which does not break out every track. Defunkt's only link to the other albums is bassist Melvin Gibbs, who also plays on Nasty. Its credits come from a single listing and may be incomplete.
